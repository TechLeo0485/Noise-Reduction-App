## What's new in VoiceStudio 1.0.0

**Other people's voices**
- Someone else talking while you are silent is now muted, instead of slowly being treated as your voice.
- Your first words after someone else spoke are no longer cut off.

**Background noise**
- Steady noise (fans, hum, air conditioning) is now removed while you talk, not only in pauses.
- "DSP only" noise control now really lowers steady noise (about 13 dB instead of 2 dB).
- Fixed a short blip of old audio when switching between noise settings.

**Works on more computers**
- VoiceStudio measures your computer and picks settings it can keep up with.
- Computers too slow for full voice isolation get light isolation instead of gaps in your voice.

**Keyboard shortcuts**
- Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V shows or hides the window; Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M mutes or unmutes your microphone.
- They work in any app. Change them under Setup → Keyboard shortcuts.

**Updates**
- VoiceStudio checks for new versions when it starts, asks before updating, and shows what's new afterwards.
  You can turn this off under Setup → Updates.
