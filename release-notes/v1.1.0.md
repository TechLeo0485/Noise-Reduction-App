## What's new in VoiceStudio 1.1.0

**Voice changer**
- The Voice page is now a voice changer: click a voice and it is used right away. Three new voices: Clear, Lighter
  and Very Deep.
- Adjust any voice with three sliders - Pitch, Thickness and Presence - and save it under your own name.
- New shortcuts that work during a call: **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+N** switches to the next voice, **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+B** turns
  the voice effects off and on. Change them in Setup.
- "Record 5 s and play back" is now on the Voice page too.

**Only my voice**
- Removing other people's voices has its own page, **Only my voice**, with your voice recording and a choice of
  isolation model: **Full isolation** (best quality), **Lightweight filter** (needs a trained model file) and
  **Voice check only** (lowest CPU). Your recorded voice works with all of them.
- Someone who starts talking after you have been quiet for a while is muted much sooner: about a third as much of
  their voice gets through.

**Background noise**
- New, experimental: **Clicks and taps** (Clean-up → Advanced noise settings) turns down mouse clicks and keys between
  your words. It is off by default.
