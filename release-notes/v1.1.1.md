## What's new in VoiceStudio 1.1.1

**Only my voice (Full isolation) - fixes**
- Your first words are no longer cut off when you start talking, even while you are clicking or typing.
- After you stop talking, other people's voices and mouse clicks no longer get through.
- Mouse clicks and key presses no longer count as someone talking, so they don't open or hold the voice gate.
- If VoiceStudio wasn't sure it was you, it now lets your voice through again sooner.

Tip: for even fewer clicks, turn on **Clicks and taps** (Clean-up → Advanced noise settings).
