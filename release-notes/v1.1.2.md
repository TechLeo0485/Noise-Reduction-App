## What's new in VoiceStudio 1.1.2

**Steadier voice isolation**
- Fewer cut-off words, both when you start talking and in the middle of a sentence. A cough, a laugh or a quiet word
  no longer mutes you; someone else's voice is still muted right away.
- VoiceStudio now runs at high priority on your processor and graphics card, so other programs (games, browsers)
  no longer make your voice drop out.
- VoiceStudio never switches to a weaker isolation mode by itself any more. The model you choose is the one that runs,
  even when the computer is busy. If the computer is too busy to keep up, VoiceStudio tells you instead.
- On processors with many cores, VoiceStudio can now use more of them for voice isolation.

Tip: if you have an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card, choose it in **Setup** to take the work off your processor.
