## What's new in VoiceStudio 1.1.3

**Fixes constant cutting with Full isolation**
- On some computers your voice kept cutting out over and over after a while. When the computer got busy,
  VoiceStudio switched to larger processing steps that needed more delay than it allowed, and then kept throwing
  away bits of your voice. It now only uses settings that really fit, and it no longer drops your voice to save
  a few milliseconds of delay.
- On a busy computer you may hear your voice arrive slightly later in rare moments, instead of hearing gaps.
