## What's new in VoiceStudio 1.1.3

**Fixes constant cutting with Full isolation**
- On some computers your voice kept cutting out over and over after a while. When the computer got busy,
  VoiceStudio switched to larger processing steps that needed more delay than it allowed, and then kept throwing
  away bits of your voice. It now only uses settings that really fit, and it no longer drops your voice to save
  a few milliseconds of delay.
- On a busy computer you may hear your voice arrive slightly later in rare moments, instead of hearing gaps.
- VoiceStudio now picks a setting with room to spare when it measures your computer, not one that only just keeps
  up when nothing else is running. If it still falls behind, it uses more processor cores first, before adding delay.
- If your computer is briefly too slow, the tiny gap is now smoothly filled from your own voice instead of
  dropping to silence, so short hiccups are much harder to hear. Your raw microphone is never used for this.
- After a busy moment (a game, an update) VoiceStudio returns to its faster setting by itself after 5 quiet
  minutes, so the extra delay does not stay for the rest of the day.
- Starting up and switching voices is quicker on computers whose graphics card is slower than the processor.
- When VoiceStudio adjusts its settings while running, it now does so without a gap, or waits for a pause
  between your sentences, so you no longer hear a short dropout when it adapts.
